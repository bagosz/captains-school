Captain's School

Learn the wargame rules one mission at a time. Each mission is built around one rule, and you only win once you understand it.

How to play
  • 1
    Pick a mission and read its rule cards.
  • 2
    Play on a table measured in inches. Hover over a target to see the rolls it needs, then watch the dice land in the tray. Zoom with the mouse wheel or the + button and drag the table to move the view.
  • 3
    Every rule in play has a chip: click it for a short explanation and a link to the Rules Manual. The first time a rule comes into play, a popup explains it.
