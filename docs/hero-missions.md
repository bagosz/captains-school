# Heroes: design of the second part of the campaign

Status: mission 18 teaches Stand Fast and Terror, mission 20 Heroic Move (design mission 8). Stages A (Might, Will, Fate; mission 17), B and C (universal and specialist Heroic Actions) and Stand Fast from stage D
have been in the engine since 13.10.2026 (`docs/heroes-engine.md`, the `?sandbox` sandbox). The missions that teach them are waiting to be written;
the mission numbering has shifted (`docs/curriculum.md`, chapter V).
Rules source: Rules Manual 2024 (PDF pages in brackets). Profiles: 2024 army lists (Army of Edoras, Usurpers of Edoras).

## 1. What you need to know to play Heroes

| Mechanic | Key points in 2024 | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Might: changing a die** | +1 to one die per point, max 6. Used at the very end, after re-rolls and modifiers. Only on the Hero's own rolls: Duel Roll, To Hit, To Wound, In The Way, Courage and Intelligence tests, Casting/Resist, Fate roll. Not on a roll-off to break a tie. | p. 77–78 |
| **Bidding Might in a duel** | When two Heroes fight, the one who is losing spends Might first; then the other; and so on in turn. | p. 77 (example 59) |
| **Declaring actions** | The "Declare Heroic Actions" step at the start of the Move, Shoot and Fight phases. The player **without** Priority starts, then they alternate until both pass. One Hero: one action per phase. Cost: 1 Might. | p. 79 |
| **Order of opposing actions** | When both sides have a Heroic Move or Combat: the player with Priority rolls a D6, on 1–3 Evil starts, on 4+ Good, then they alternate. | p. 80 |
| **Universal actions** | Heroic Move (+ the *With Me* shout), Heroic Shoot (+ *Loose*), Heroic Combat. Every Hero has them. | p. 81–82 |
| **Specialist actions** | March (+3″, no Charge, *At the Double*), Accuracy (re-roll In The Way, *Take Aim*), Strike (+D3 to FV), Defence (wounds only on a natural 6), Strength (double S), Challenge (+1 A and +1 To Wound against the challenged model), Resolve (automatic Broken test, an extra Resist die). Each profile has its own list. | p. 83–86 |
| **Will** | +1 to a Courage test per point (can be mixed with Might). Casting and resisting magical powers. | p. 87 |
| **Fate** | When a Hero takes a wound: spend a point, roll a D6, on 4+ the wound does not go through. Further points one at a time. Might can raise the Fate roll. | p. 88 |
| **Courage** | Test: 2D6 ≥ C. Terror: a test before a Charge, a failure means no move this turn. | p. 60, 131 |
| **Broken and Stand Fast** | An army is Broken when at the start of the turn losses > half of the starting number of models. Every activated model takes a Courage test, a failure means fleeing. A Hero who passes calls *Stand Fast*: Warriors within 6″ and in line of sight pass automatically. | p. 61 |
| **Heroic Tiers** | Legend, Valour, Fortitude, Minor (plus Independent). Important for Challenge: the target must be of the same tier or higher. | p. 157 |

## 3. Missions: the "Heroes of Edoras" campaign (missions 7–15)

The rule is as in part one: one new mechanic per mission, and the mission can be won only after understanding it.

**7. A Captain's Might: Might on the dice and Fate**
- Captain of Rohan + 4 Warriors of Rohan against Hill Tribes Chieftain + 6 Hill Tribesmen.
- Goal: kill the Chieftain, and your Captain must survive.
- Teaching: after a roll you click your Hero's die in the tray and raise it by 1 for 1 Might. A duel of two Heroes is a bidding (the loser spends first). When the Captain takes a wound, a Fate roll (4+) is offered.
- Catch: the Captain has only 2 Might. If it is spent too early on an ordinary Warrior, there will not be enough against the Chieftain.

**8. With Me!: Heroic Move**
- Scenario rule: the Usurpers have Priority in turns 1–3 (ambush).
- Captain of Rohan with a unit must take the ford before the Chieftain's band. A Heroic Move with *With Me* lets the Warriors within 6″ move first together with him.
- The Chieftain can also declare a Heroic Move, and then a D6 decides who moves first (p. 80).
- Goal: at the end of turn 4, have more models at the ford.

**9. At the Double: Heroic March**
- Captain of Rohan leads 5 Warriors to the gate of Edoras (30″) in 3 turns, and the Chieftain with his band chases them.
- March gives +3″ to everyone within 6″, but no Charging is allowed and the move must end within 6″ of the Hero.
- Teaching: when to use March instead of Move and why you cannot Charge with it.

**10. Loose! / Take Aim: Heroic Shoot and Heroic Accuracy**
- Háma (SV 3+) + Warriors of Rohan with bows against Hill Tribesmen archers hidden in undergrowth; the enemy has Priority.
- Heroic Shoot (*Loose*) lets you shoot before the enemy; Heroic Accuracy (*Take Aim*) re-rolls failed In The Way.
- Might on an In The Way test is exactly example 58 from the rulebook (Háma and the wall).

**11. Heroic Combat: breaking through the line**
- Haleth (3 Might) must reach General Targg behind the Hill Tribesmen line in 3 turns.
- Heroic Combat resolves his fight first; if he kills all enemies in the combat, he can move and Charge again.
- Heroic Strike (+D3 to FV) helps win duels against several opponents.
- Bonus: Targg has Master of Battle (4+), so he can copy a Heroic Action declared near him.

**12. Unbreakable: Heroic Defence against Heroic Strength**
- Háma defends the pass between the rocks for 4 turns against Freca and his men.
- Freca with Heroic Strength has S8, so he wounds Háma (D6) on 3+. Heroic Defence makes Háma take wounds only on a natural 6, regardless of Strength.
- Teaching: action against action and saving Fate for the worst moment.

**13. The Challenge: Haleth against Wulf**
- Heroic Challenge (target within 6″, the same Valour tier): +1 Attack and +1 To Wound, the challenged model accepts or refuses, and the killer regains 1 Might.
- Wulf has Strike, Strength, Challenge and 3 Might, so every duel involves bidding.
- Goal: kill Wulf.

**14. Hold Fast: Courage, Broken, Stand Fast, Terror**
- Your army starts right at the Broken threshold. Every model takes a Courage test on activation (2D6 in the tray), and a failure means fleeing.
- A Hero who passes the test calls *Stand Fast*. Will adds +1 to the test, and Heroic Resolve (Haleth) passes automatically.
- In addition a Mordor Troll with Terror: a test before charging it.
- Goal: hold the gate until the end of turn 6.

**15. The Hammerhand: everything at once**
- Helm Hammerhand (a free Heroic Combat in every Fight phase thanks to Into the Fray), Háma, Haleth and Warriors against Wulf, Freca, Targg, Hill Tribesmen and Rohirrim Traitors.
- The AI also declares Heroic Actions.

## 5. Interface ideas

- **Might in the tray:** after the dice stop, the ones belonging to the Hero get a gold outline. A click adds +1 (the die changes its result, the Might counter drops), and a "Done" button closes the window. The window appears only when Might can change something, so the game does not slow down needlessly.
- **Hero panel:** M/W/F as point tokens, a list of available actions with a short description and a rule label (a link to the rulebook page).
- **Declaration step:** a "Declare Heroic Actions" bar with action buttons for each Hero and a "Pass" button. The enemy's declarations appear in the log and above its token.
- **Shouts** (*With Me*, *At the Double*, *Loose*, *Take Aim*): after the declaration, the 6″ range and the models affected by the shout are highlighted on the board.
